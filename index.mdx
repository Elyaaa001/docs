## What is Whelp AI?

Whelp AI is a conversational customer support platform designed to help businesses communicate more effectively and efficiently with their customers across various channels. It allows companies to connect with customers through preferred channels such as SMS, voice, WhatsApp, WeChat, Messenger, email, and live chat. Some key features of Whelp include:
AI-Powered Automation: The platform's chatbot can automate up to 60% of customer inquiries, allowing teams to focus on more complex issues.
Multichannel Support: Businesses can communicate with customers wherever they are, ensuring a seamless experience.
Analytics and Reporting: Whelp provides tools to track and analyze performance and customer interactions.
Integration Capabilities: The platform can easily integrate with a wide range of tools and platforms, streamlining workflows.
Founded in New York, Whelp currently supports over 550 companies across 16 countries. It is part of the Techstars portfolio and the 500 Startups accelerator program, gaining access to valuable resources and support as it continues to grow.
