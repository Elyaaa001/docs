## What is Whelp AI?

Whelp AI is a conversational customer support platform designed to help businesses communicate more effectively and efficiently with their customers across various channels. It enables companies to connect with users through their preferred platforms — including SMS, voice, WhatsApp, WeChat, Messenger, email, and live chat — ensuring a unified and seamless experience.

### Key Features:
- **AI-Powered Automation**: Whelp’s chatbot can automate up to 60% of customer inquiries, allowing support teams to focus on more complex tasks.  
- **Multichannel Support**: Engage with customers wherever they are, from messaging apps to traditional email and live chat.  
- **Analytics & Reporting**: Gain insights into performance and customer behavior with powerful analytics tools.  
- **Integration Capabilities**: Easily integrate Whelp with your existing tools and platforms to streamline workflows and improve efficiency.

By leveraging Chatbase, businesses can provide 24/7 support, engage users in meaningful conversations, and automate routine tasks, leading to increased efficiency and customer satisfaction.


## Make it yours

To integrate your AI assistant into your website using Whelp, follow these simple steps:

- **Design Your Chatbot**: Use Whelp’s intuitive drag-and-drop builder to create a tailored chatbot that reflects your brand and meets your specific customer service needs.
- **Enable Automation**: Configure your chatbot to automatically handle common customer inquiries 24/7, ensuring prompt and consistent responses at all times.
- **Integrate Seamlessly**: Embed the chatbot into your website with just a few clicks. Whelp supports multichannel integration, allowing you to engage customers through their preferred communication platforms.
- **Monitor & Optimize**: After deployment, use Whelp’s analytics tools to track your chatbot’s performance. Leverage insights to continuously improve the experience and effectiveness of your AI assistant.
