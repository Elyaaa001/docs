## What is Whelp AI?

Whelp AI is a conversational customer support platform designed to help businesses communicate more effectively and efficiently with their customers across various channels. It enables companies to connect with users through their preferred platforms — including SMS, voice, WhatsApp, WeChat, Messenger, email, and live chat — ensuring a unified and seamless experience.

### Key Features:
- **Omnichannel Support**: Reach customers through various channels such as Live Chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Email, and Social Media, ensuring seamless and consistent communication.
- **AI-Powered Automation**: Automate up to 60% of customer inquiries, enabling support agents to focus on more complex and high-priority tasks.
- **Analytics and Reporting**: Access advanced tools to track, measure, and analyze customer interactions and team performance.
- **Integration Capabilities**: Connect effortlessly with a wide range of external tools and applications to streamline operations.
- **Customizable Communication Campaigns**: Design and automate personalized campaigns via WhatsApp, Telegram, Email, and Facebook Messenger.
- **Unified Inbox**: Manage conversations from phone, email, chat, and messaging apps in a single, centralized interface.
- **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)**: Maintain detailed customer profiles and manage interactions efficiently across touchpoints.
- **Real-Time Collaboration**: Collaborate instantly with team members to resolve issues and improve service delivery.
- **Customizable Data Management**: Organize and track customer data according to your business-specific criteria and workflows.
- **Security Features**: Protect customer information with advanced encryption, secure authentication, and role-based access controls.

By leveraging Whelp AI, businesses can provide round-the-clock customer support, automate up to 60% of routine inquiries, and engage customers across multiple channels with intelligent, human-like conversations—driving efficiency, consistency, and higher satisfaction at scale.


## Make it yours

To integrate your AI assistant into your website using Whelp, follow these simple steps:

- **Design Your Chatbot**: Use Whelp’s intuitive drag-and-drop builder to create a tailored chatbot that reflects your brand and meets your specific customer service needs.
- **Enable Automation**: Configure your chatbot to automatically handle common customer inquiries 24/7, ensuring prompt and consistent responses at all times.
- **Integrate Seamlessly**: Embed the chatbot into your website with just a few clicks. Whelp supports multichannel integration, allowing you to engage customers through their preferred communication platforms.
- **Monitor & Optimize**: After deployment, use Whelp’s analytics tools to track your chatbot’s performance. Leverage insights to continuously improve the experience and effectiveness of your AI assistant.
